**Goal:** Determine whether prior matched-template support and time since a prior emitted detector decision contextualize the existing energy comparison consistently beyond development record 228. No classifier, combined score, threshold selection, detector intervention, or repeated tuning.

**Architecture:** Add read-only prior emission context to the research replay/OpportunityMonitor hook, before the current pipeline-step emission learns or becomes previous. **Tech Stack:** Node.js ES modules, node:test, existing local WFDB replay, frozen 101-record `validation.json`, independent original/control and cap2 shadow banks. No external APIs or nested agents.

## Frozen protocol — recorded before the first context result run

### Measurements and causality

- Freeze enumeration, alignment, query availability, greedy posthoc miss pairing and all scoring. Keep four templates, signed correlation 0.90, maturity three **prior** observations, expiry 30 s, +/-80 ms vectors, energy/shape EMA 0.125. Queries never teach, refresh, expire, or change status/events.
- `supportBefore` and template `ageS` are copied from the existing query, never recomputed after an emission. All mature rows must retain support >=3; report status/cold-start populations separately from the mature comparison.
- Prior emission = most recent valid detector event available **strictly before `queriedAt` in decision order**, whether or not its shape could be learned. Include warmup emissions; clear previous emission at a gap. Exclude the current pipeline-step event even if its estimated `event.t` precedes the query. No future event lookup or reference-derived online feature.
- **Primary clock:** `elapsedDecisionS = queriedAt - previous.emittedAt`; when present it must be finite and strictly positive (therefore nonnegative), as the current step is excluded. Preserve prior `event.t` and `emittedAt` distinctly in ephemeral query context.
- Diagnostic only: `signedEstimatedS = candidate.t - previous.event.t`. It may be negative for delayed opportunities. Report negative/zero/positive/missing counts and signed quantiles; never call it nonnegative refractory elapsed or substitute it for the primary clock.
- Missing prior emission is a separate stratum, not infinity/zero/imputed time. Report all-query missing/cold-start/status cross-counts and mature missing counts. Warmup and EOF retain the original rules; no future completion at EOF.

### Fixed strata and populations

- Primary decision-clock bins, left inclusive/right exclusive: `[0,0.22)`, `[0.22,0.36)`, `[0.36,1)`, `[1,infinity)`, plus `missing`. 0.22 s is the current detector refractory parameter; 0.36 s is the earlier descriptive post-complex screen; 1 s is purely descriptive. These bins are **not acceptance/rejection criteria**, and decision time is not the detector's estimated-sample refractory clock.
- Prior support bins: `3–5`, `6–15`, `>=16`. Report the 15 support/time joint cells (empty cells omitted from compact JSON but defined here), plus energy-conditioned context using the prior energy histogram edges `[-4,-2,-1,0,1,2,4]` on `L=log2(query/prior energy)`.
- **Primary:** mature proposals paired one-to-one to baseline misses versus `noReferenceNeighborhood` unmatched proposals. **Secondary, separate:** paired versus `alreadyDetectedNeighborhood`, versus `duplicateMissNeighborhood`, and versus all unmatched. Neighborhood labels enter only posthoc, not prior state.
- All 101 records are official, including all difficult 108/207 intervals. Keep records without paired proposals and without mature matches. Excluding 228 is a **diagnostic projection only**, never exclusion from official totals.

### Descriptions, denominators, and closure

- Per bank and per record: raw mature paired/other counts, energy and context available denominators, fixed quantiles (`round((n-1)p)`), support/time cell occupancy by group, age/support/elapsed/signed distributions and missing/negative counts. Energy comparisons retain pooled AUC `P(L_paired > L_other)` (ties 0.5), both p10–p90 overlap fractions, within-template mixture/one-sided counts, pair-count-weighted AUC, and lists for templates with >=3 ratios per group. Higher energy is the prior hypothesis, not a direction chosen after seeing context.
- Higher decision elapsed is the prespecified duplicate-burden hypothesis: report `P(elapsed_paired > elapsed_other)` (ties 0.5), overlap, within-template statistics, and within fixed energy bins. Support has **no presumed monotonic direction**: report fixed-bin frequencies/quantiles and paired-minus-other bin proportions, not a posthoc directed support AUC. Conditional energy distributions/AUC use support, time and joint strata. No manufactured multifeature score.
- Conditional cells disclose raw n, usable n, group overlap, and template/record eligibility. AUC on any nonempty pair is descriptive; evidence eligibility always requires >=3 observations in **each** comparison group (and usable values for the statistic). Unequal denominators/missing state are explicit.
- **Frozen closure criterion (control primary bank):** evidence of consistent context beyond 228 requires at least **three non-228 records**, each with >=3 pairs and >=3 no-reference comparators with both valid energy and prior decision distance; energy AUC >0.5 and elapsed AUC >0.5 must agree in **every** eligible record. Additionally at least one of the same fixed joint support/time cells must have >=3 usable values per group in >=3 non-228 records, with energy AUC >0.5 in each of its eligible records. Cap2 is separately reported, not substituted to pass control. If record or joint support is insufficient, close **inconclusive**, not disproven. If sufficient but direction disagrees, close **not consistent under this descriptive criterion**. Passing would mean consistent association only, **not proven multifeature incremental predictive performance**; no fitted model or held-out test exists.
- Marginal/conditional energy AUC differences contextualize overlap, not causal or predictive incremental validation. Support enrichment can inform descriptions but cannot rescue failed sample/elapsed closure. Dependence among opportunities, alignment discrepancy, development-corpus bias, sparse cells and 228 dominance must be disclosed.
